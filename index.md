## About me 

- I am currently building something new (Stealth), focusing on agentic AI and LLM systems, RL post-training, and distributed training and inference.
- I am a PhD (new grad) in Computer Science from Simon Fraser University, supervised by Prof. Jiannan Wang and Prof. Jian Pei. 
- I obtained a master's and a bachelor's degree in Information Engineering at Zhejiang University, supervised by Prof. Yingming Li and Prof. Zhongfei (Mark) Zhang.
- Interests:
  - LLM Infrastructure: Two years of experience in large-scale LLM system topics, including exploring failure recovery, disaggregation, and SLO-aware serving.
  - ML for OR: Exploring how ML could enhance operations research.
